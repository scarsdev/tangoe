Tangoe for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Tangoe helps organisations schedule staff, record verified work hours and share approved job updates. We collect only the information needed to provide those features. We do not sell personal information, run advertising, or track people across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Tangoe is an iOS app operated by Jesse Williams (“Tangoe”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This policy explains how Tangoe handles information when owners, staff and invited customers use the app.

Contact: studiogeneral@icloud.com

2. Information Tangoe collects

Account and organisation information

  • Email address and authentication identifiers, including Sign in with Apple identifiers where used.
  • Organisation membership, role, staff invitations and customer access invitations.
  • Organisation, project, job-site and shift information entered by authorised users.

Work, shift and verification records

  • Scheduled shift times and recorded start, break, resume and finish events.
  • Precise location coordinates during an active work session, together with location time, horizontal accuracy, speed, heading and distance from the assigned site.
  • Motion category, battery percentage, network type, app version and an app-scoped device identifier used to assess and sign work-session evidence.
  • Job notes, progress status, materials, issues, generated reports and consent or approval records.

Content you choose to provide

  • Photos selected from your library or captured with the camera.
  • Voice recordings and speech-to-text transcripts.
  • Feedback, support messages and any other information you submit.

Notifications and technical information

  • Apple Push Notification service device tokens and notification preferences.
  • Limited service logs needed to secure, operate and troubleshoot the app, such as request time, error and network information processed by our service providers.

Tangoe does not currently include third-party advertising, behavioural tracking, or third-party analytics and crash-reporting SDKs.

3. When collection happens

Tangoe requests location only while you prepare or record an active work session. Location and motion monitoring stop when the session stops. Camera, photo-library, microphone and speech-recognition access are used only when you choose the corresponding progress-proof feature. Notification permission is optional.

iOS presents permission controls before protected data is accessed. You can deny or later revoke a permission in iOS Settings, although the related feature may then be unavailable or produce a partially verified record.

4. How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and protect access to organisations, jobs and customer connections.
  • Schedule shifts and calculate gross, break and verified working time.
  • Confirm whether work-session evidence is consistent with an assigned job site.
  • Create tamper-evident event records, progress proofs and daily reports.
  • Share only approved updates and issued reports with authorised customers.
  • Send requested operational notifications and invitation links.
  • Prevent misuse, investigate faults, maintain security and provide support.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

5. Sharing and service providers

We disclose information only as needed for the app to operate:

  • Authorised Tangoe users. Owners and authorised organisation members can access records within their organisation. Staff see assigned work. Customers see only the updates and reports approved for their connection, and that access can expire or be revoked.
  • Supabase. Tangoe uses Supabase for authentication, hosted database, file storage, realtime updates and server functions. Supabase processes information on our behalf under its privacy terms.
  • Apple. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, device permissions, push notifications, location services and speech-recognition services. Depending on device and OS settings, speech recognition and notification data may be processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce rights, protect users or investigate fraud and security incidents.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Storage, security and international processing

Tangoe uses access controls, encrypted network connections, expiring private-media links and signed event evidence. Authentication sessions are stored in the device Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate contractual and legal safeguards for those transfers.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain account, organisation and work records while they are needed to operate Tangoe, maintain an organisation’s agreed work history, meet legal or business record-keeping requirements, resolve disputes and protect the service. Retention can therefore vary by record and organisation.

Push tokens and personal profile details are removed when an account is deleted. Workplace evidence already shared with an organisation, including associated media, may be retained in de-identified form where needed for agreed work history, legal record-keeping or legitimate claims. Residual copies may remain temporarily in secure backups before being overwritten.

You can delete your account inside Tangoe under Settings or Workspace & Sync by choosing “Delete Account”. This immediately revokes the login, removes device and notification data, and de-identifies the personal profile. For help with deletion or a follow-up privacy request, email studiogeneral@icloud.com from the address connected to your account. We may need to verify your identity.

8. Your choices and consent

  • Change location, camera, photo, microphone, speech and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Change notification preferences inside Tangoe.
  • End a customer connection using its revoke control.
  • Delete your account inside Tangoe from Settings or Workspace & Sync.
  • Ask your organisation owner to correct or remove organisation-managed work information.
  • Request access, correction or deletion by contacting us.

9. Children

Tangoe is a workplace and project-management service and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through consumer-directed features.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Tangoe’s features, providers or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by another appropriate method.

11. Contact

Questions, privacy requests and account-deletion requests can be sent to studiogeneral@icloud.com.
